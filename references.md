# References and Influences

Life Systems is an original methodology developed by Matthew McGilvery for everyday decision architecture.

The core Life Systems loop is:

`Problem → Complexity → Decision → Execution → Audit`

The framework helps people diagnose friction, map complexity, choose responses, execute within real limits, and audit whether the original issue was resolved. It was developed through creative practice, independent work, and personal systems design, but it is not limited to those contexts.

## Credited Frameworks

### Cynefin Framework

The Complexity Mapping module adapts concepts from the Cynefin framework, which is associated with Dave Snowden, Cognitive Edge, and The Cynefin Company.

Life Systems does not claim ownership of Cynefin, its domain model, or its original terminology. References to Clear, Complicated, Complex, Chaotic, Confusion or Disorder, and related action patterns are included as credited sense-making concepts within a broader Life Systems methodology.

Reference sources:

- The Cynefin Company: https://thecynefin.co/
- Cynefin wiki: https://cynefin.io/wiki/Main_Page
- Trademark reference: https://trademarks.justia.com/875/76/cynefin-87576158.html

### Lean Canvas

The `lean_canvas.md` file uses the Lean Canvas format as a strategic planning reference structure. Lean Canvas was created by Ash Maurya and is commonly described as a startup-focused adaptation of the Business Model Canvas.

Life Systems does not claim ownership of Lean Canvas or its original structure. The content inside the Life Systems canvas is original positioning for this framework.

Reference sources:

- Ash Maurya: https://www.leanfoundry.com/about
- LEANSTACK: https://www.leanstack.com/
- Lean Canvas reference: https://bmtoolbox.net/tools/lean-canvas/
